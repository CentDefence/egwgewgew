Pirlos Futbol League

Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 22, 2026

1. Introduction. This Privacy Policy explains what information the PFL Bot (the “Bot”) of Pirlos Futbol League (“PFL”) collects, how it is used, and what choices you have. We keep data collection to the minimum needed for the Bot to work. By using the Bot you agree to the practices described here. 2. Information We Collect. To run signings, transfers and rosters, the Bot stores the following: Discord user IDs of players, managers and assistant managers; Discord server (guild) IDs; role IDs for clubs and staff roles; the transfers channel ID; the emoji chosen for each club; and each member’s club, staff status and contract length (number of seasons). When a command is used, the Bot also handles the text you enter, such as the reason for a release or for leaving a club, so that it can be shown in the announcement. 3. Information We Do Not Collect. The Bot does not read, store or analyse your normal chat messages. It does not collect email addresses, passwords, payment details, phone numbers, real names or locations. It does not track you across other servers or websites. 4. How We Use Information. We use the information only to provide the Bot’s features: to show club rosters, to deliver contract offers and transfer requests, to assign club and staff roles, and to post live transfer announcements in the channel chosen by your server. We do not use your information for advertising, profiling or any unrelated purpose. 5. Direct Messages. The Bot sends you direct messages when you receive a contract offer, a transfer offer, or the result of a request you made. These messages contain the club name, contract length and the name of the manager who sent the offer. The Bot does not read replies you send to it in direct messages. You respond only by using the buttons. 6. Public Announcements. Signings, releases, transfers and appointments are announced publicly in the transfers channel of the server. These announcements show your Discord mention, the club, and where relevant the reason or contract length. Please keep this in mind before using the commands. 7. Data Storage and Security. Data is kept in a database on the system that hosts the Bot. We take reasonable steps to protect it, but no method of storage or transmission is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security. 8. Data Sharing. We do not sell, rent or trade your information. We do not share it with third parties, except where it is required by law or needed to protect the safety of the league and its members. Information shown in announcements and rosters is visible to members of the server where it is posted. 9. Data Retention. Roster and contract data is kept for as long as it is needed for the league and the Bot to operate. When you leave a club, are released, or leave the server, the related roster record is removed. If the Bot is removed from a server, that server’s data may be deleted. 10. Your Rights and Choices. You may ask to see, correct or delete the data the Bot holds about you. You can also leave your club with the Bot’s commands, or block direct messages in your Discord privacy settings, although this will stop offers from reaching you. To make a request, contact the PFL staff using the details below. 11. Children’s Privacy. The Bot is only intended for people who are old enough to use Discord under its own rules. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone below that age. If you believe a child has used the Bot, please contact us and we will remove the data. 12. Third-Party Services. The Bot runs on Discord and relies on its platform. Discord processes your data under its own Terms of Service and Privacy Policy, which are separate from this one, and we do not control them. 13. Legal Basis for Using Information. We use the limited information described in this policy because it is needed to provide the features you ask the Bot to perform, such as sending a contract or updating a roster, and because it is in the legitimate interest of the league to keep an accurate record of its clubs and members. Where the law requires your consent, using the Bot’s commands and buttons is treated as your agreement to this use. 14. Data Minimisation. We aim to collect as little data as possible. The Bot stores only identifiers and settings, not personal profiles. We do not ask you to give any information that is not needed for a command to work, and we do not build detailed records about your behaviour, interests or activity on Discord. 15. Automatic Role Handling. The Bot may read the roles a member has in a server in order to work out which club the member belongs to and whether the member is a player, an assistant manager or a manager. This happens automatically when roles change. It is used only to keep the roster correct, and it does not involve any decisions that have legal or similar significant effects on you. 16. Logs and Technical Information. The system that hosts the Bot may keep technical logs, for example error messages and connection details, which can contain Discord IDs. These logs are used only to find and fix problems, to keep the Bot stable, and to protect it from abuse. They are not used for any other purpose and are deleted or overwritten regularly. 17. Cookies and Website Tracking. This website is a simple information page. It does not use advertising cookies, analytics trackers, social media plugins or any tools that follow you around the internet. Your browser or hosting provider may process basic technical data, such as your IP address, in order to deliver the page, which is outside our control. 18. Backups. To protect against data loss, the Bot’s database may be copied from time to time. Backups are stored with the same care as the main data and are removed or replaced after a reasonable period. Information that has been deleted from the active database may therefore remain in a backup for a short time before it is overwritten. 19. International Data Transfers. Discord and the system that hosts the Bot may be located in different countries from the one where you live. By using the Bot you understand that your information may be processed in other countries, which may have different data protection rules from your own. We take reasonable steps to keep it protected wherever it is processed. 20. Server Administrators. Administrators of each Discord server decide how the Bot is set up in their server, including which roles count as staff, which clubs exist and in which channel announcements appear. Information posted in that channel is visible to everyone who can see it, and the administrators of that server are responsible for who has access to it. 21. Your Rights Under Data Protection Laws. Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access the information held about you, to have it corrected or deleted, to limit or object to its use, and to receive a copy of it. You may also have the right to complain to your local data protection authority. We will respond to reasonable requests as soon as we can and may ask you to confirm your Discord identity first. 22. How to Request Deletion. To have your information removed, contact the PFL staff through the official Pirlos Futbol League Discord server and tell us your Discord username or ID. We will remove your roster and contract records from the Bot’s database where possible. Removing your club role in Discord, leaving your club with the Bot’s commands, or leaving the server will also remove your active roster record. 23. Data Breaches. If we become aware of a security problem that is likely to put your information at serious risk, we will take steps to contain it and will inform the affected servers or members through the official PFL Discord server where this is appropriate or required by law. 24. Public Information on Discord. Your Discord username, display name, avatar and mention are already visible to other members according to your Discord settings. The Bot uses these only to show the right person in a message, a roster or an announcement. If you want to limit what others can see, you should change your settings directly in Discord. 25. Changes to This Policy. We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The latest version will always be available on this page with its “Last updated” date. Continued use of the Bot after a change means you accept the updated policy. 26. Contact. If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or want your data removed, please contact the PFL staff through the official Pirlos Futbol League Discord server.
